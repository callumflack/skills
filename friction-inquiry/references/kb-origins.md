# KB origins and scope history

Use this to distinguish the source of the skill from later interpretations. The local KB note titles below are provenance pointers, not runtime dependencies or a required diagnostic lens.

## Callum's account of the original use

In the 7 October 2026 revision request, Callum described being deeply frustrated with Vana's product manager. He wanted to record himself talking through the issues, then use the skill to understand the actual problem. He described this as knowledge work and included friction while collaborating with agents.

This is Callum's present account of the origin. The original recording or originating conversation was not available in this revision. The request also said he had used the skill little and thought it might be important. It establishes intended use, not demonstrated effectiveness.

## What the repository records

- Commit `5381f21` added the skill on 24 June 2026. Its triggers included irritated team, product, code, and work objections. Its procedure already ended in a decision-change ask and team-usable message.
- Commit `d3a4436` narrowed the scope on 9 September 2026 to interpersonal conflict. It explicitly excluded agent-process problems and ordinary technical or product criticism.

The Git history establishes those written framings. It does not establish that either framing captured the original spoken use. The current revision follows Callum's clarification rather than restoring the first committed procedure unchanged.

## Relevant local notes

- [[Make the miss visible before asking to be understood — Synthesis E7]] links irritation to tacit competence, proxies, ownership, and making a consequential miss inspectable. Its operating rule aims at changing a team decision.
- [[Taste is tacit know-how seen from outside — E7]] distinguishes articulated knowledge from practice-formed judgment that may precede explanation. It emphasizes the artifact and the encounter, correction, and repetition through which judgment forms.
- [[Armin Ronacher & Cristina Poncela Cubeiro, Earendil — The Friction is Your Judgment E6]] concerns agent-assisted engineering. It includes the mismatch between producing output and carrying responsibility for its quality.
- [[Own the node where context is born — Inquiry E7]] asks how knowledge retains the context in which it formed.

These notes support investigating concrete cues and preserving the context of an objection. They do not make every objection interpersonal or require every inquiry to end in persuasion.

## Limits of the inherited framing

The earlier reference also drew on [[READ Daniel Kazandjian — The Neuroscience of Enlightenment  Kathryn Devaney E7]], [[Respect can be paid, but not extracted — Claim E6]], [[Side doors work by making usefulness legible — Claim]], and [[We are how we solve problems — identity as obstacle to learning E7]]. It interpreted anger through recognition, identity, and old threat filters.

That was an interpretive frame, not evidence about the person using the skill. The revised procedure does not infer a wound, trauma, or identity problem from frustration. It also does not require proof of a technical failure before examining trust, respect, or power.

## Current design choice

Use the account to recover the concern, distinguish observations from interpretations, and develop a reading that can be examined. Preserve what matters in the source while allowing its explanation to change.

A private working understanding can finish the task. Evidence serves that understanding first. A decision challenge or message is a possible later use, selected by the user's purpose.
