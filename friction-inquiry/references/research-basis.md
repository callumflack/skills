# Research background

Read this only when the user asks about the intellectual basis. During a live account, start with the account.

These are reading pointers inherited from the original skill. They are not a validation study of friction-inquiry. The October 2026 revision did not independently recheck the external papers or their earlier summaries.

## Questions for the readings

The useful questions are how to recover judgment from concrete incidents, how to examine an interpretation without dismissing the concern, and how to move from understanding to action when action is wanted.

The earlier skill used this bibliography to justify a decision-change protocol. Those workflow implications were author interpretations. They did not establish that every inquiry should end in a proof object, a recommendation, or a team message.

## Eliciting tacit judgment

Hoffman, Crandall, and Shadbolt, "Use of the Critical Decision Method to Elicit Expert Knowledge," Human Factors, 1998.

[Paper](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1518/001872098779480442).

This is the inherited reference for inquiry through incidents, cues, and decision requirements. Consult the paper before making claims about the method. The current skill's spoken-account workflow is a design choice, not a claimed implementation or validated extension of that method.

## Emotion and judgment

The original bibliography included these papers:

- Schmitt, Gielnik, and Seibel, "When and how does anger during goal pursuit relate to goal achievement? The roles of persistence and action planning," Motivation and Emotion, 2019. [Paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11031-018-9720-4).
- Lerner and Tiedens, "Portrait of the Angry Decision Maker: How Appraisal Tendencies Shape Anger's Influence on Cognition," Journal of Behavioral Decision Making, 2006. [Paper](https://projects.iq.harvard.edu/sites/projects.iq.harvard.edu/files/lernerlab/files/lerner_tiedens_2006.pdf).
- Lerner, Li, Valdesolo, and Kassam, "Emotion and Decision Making," Annual Review of Psychology, 2015. [Paper](https://scholar.harvard.edu/files/jenniferlerner/files/lerner.li_.valdesolo.kassam_in_press_annual_review_emotion_and_decision_making_edited_proof.9.29.2014.pdf).
- Lindebaum and Gabriel, "Anger and Organization Studies: From Social Disorder to Moral Order," Organization Studies, 2016. [Paper](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0170840616640848), [repository record](https://orca.cardiff.ac.uk/id/eprint/89376/).

These pointers can support a separate research inquiry about emotion and judgment. They do not let the agent infer that a particular user's anger is accurate, biased, morally justified, or evidence of a personal wound. Examine the present account and its evidence.

## Proof limits

The skill combines source elicitation, interpretation, and evidence checking as a practical knowledge-work procedure. The bibliography does not establish its effectiveness. A coherent synthesis, a well-formed note, or a successful formatting check does not prove that the underlying issue has been correctly understood.
