# Artifact shapes

Use a shape only when it helps the user examine the problem. Omit empty fields. These are private working notes unless the user requests an audience-facing output.

## First reading

Use this for a short account or an early pass through a recording. Write sentences rather than asking the user to fill in the fields.

```markdown
The concern I hear:

What in your account points to it:

Why it matters:

What I am inferring:

The question that would most change this reading:
```

## Working understanding

Use this when the account contains enough material for a fuller synthesis.

```markdown
## Working understanding

What happened:

What you expected or were protecting:

The mismatch and its consequence:

The underlying issue as I currently understand it:

What supports that reading:

What remains uncertain or contradicts it:

What would distinguish another consequential reading:
```

Keep reported events distinct from verified artifacts. Include a source phrase, incident, or timestamp when the interpretation depends on it. Multiple incidents can support one pattern, but repetition alone does not establish its cause.

## Evidence check

Use this when the next useful step is inquiry.

```markdown
Claim to examine:

Evidence available now:

Missing observation:

Smallest useful check:

What each possible result would change:
```

A recollected incident, user path, instruction and response, diff, screenshot, trace, or table of cases may serve. Choose by the claim it can examine. Do not create an artifact merely to fill the note.

## Decision or message

Use only when the user wants to move from understanding to a decision or communication. The issue may still be unresolved.

```markdown
What we can currently say:

Evidence or concrete example:

Consequence that matters:

Proposed next step and why it follows:

How we would know that step helped:
```

For a message, adapt this to the actual audience and preserve the necessary force. Separate what happened from your explanation of it. Add an owner, deadline, or acceptance test only when the proposed action needs one. Leave the text as a draft unless sending is separately authorized.
