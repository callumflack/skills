# Reframe

Find a useful change in how we understand or approach the work. Develop what the question opens up. The question may be answered by the agent, asked of the human, or explored together.

## Start from this situation

Establish what matters, the current approach, and the assumption worth reconsidering. Preserve the source's live distinctions. For a clear task with a settled method, proceed with the task unless a reframe was requested or a concrete obstacle warrants one.

For Callum's work, open the existing Homebase `Learning/README.md` and find fitting cases before proposing a new method. Read [Homebase learning](references/homebase-learning.md) for the route, retrieval command, and capture connection. Open the relevant case's story, evidence, scope and exceptions; mentioning the research note alone does not use the case. If no case fits, say so and continue from the available material. If Homebase is inaccessible, state the history gap and work from the supplied context without creating a replacement corpus.

## Choose a question and its respondent

Start with Callum’s prompt-framing collection. [Prompt collection and further questions](references/questions.md) gives the canonical KB route and published reading fallback. Read the collection and choose the perspective that could change this situation; the user need not choose a prompt or family. Preserve original wording, labelled adaptations and source attribution. The package’s further questions are optional supporting material, not a second canonical copy of those prompts.

Usually develop one useful reframe. Combine perspectives only when the situation benefits. Do not walk through the collection, score every lens, or return a menu for the user to select from. [Sources](references/sources.md) explains the distinctions behind relevant prompts.

Luu’s lens asks what we have not even considered building because an old estimate of effort has narrowed the possibilities. Assess what current capabilities make worthwhile here rather than assuming a future of free, dependable agents. Consider exact personal tools as well as larger projects; becoming worthwhile need not mean serving a wider audience. Develop the possibility before working out how to check it. Keep this distinct from making the same task faster or merely adding verification process.

Adapt the selected prompt to the situation. When connecting it to a case, retain its collection heading or existing question ID and the wording used.

- **Agent answers:** investigate methods, raw material, tools, existing systems, alternative explanations or arrangements. Do that work before returning a question to the human.
- **Human answers:** ask when the answer depends on desire, lived experience, an unspoken distinction or a consequential choice. Ask one useful question, then develop the reply. Leave an unanswered human question open.
- **Together:** offer a concrete possibility from the evidence, identify the part that depends on the human, and let their response change the approach.

Speed changes time; altitude changes the outcome we pursue; depth changes the rigor of the method; range changes the fields and techniques available. Preserve those differences. A deeper method can keep the same scope. Greater ambition can also mean making something previously unavailable, removing a burden or choosing different work.

## Work through the change

Apply the selected question to an actual detail. Develop an alternative, example, experiment or revised request and show what changed from the starting approach. A question list alone is useful only when a list was requested.

Treat unfamiliar methods as possibilities to investigate. Role names do not supply those methods. When the proposal depends on new access or tools, identify the missing capability rather than assuming it exists. Keep a proposed larger outcome separate from the work already authorized.

For creative exploration, follow what becomes interesting without forcing a business case or a verdict. Where someone must rely on the result, establish the relevant external check. A smaller or unchanged approach can be the useful conclusion.

## Return the learning

When a writable session yields a consequential discovery, successful approach, correction or unresolved question worth retaining, use the installed `capture-learning` skill and its existing Homebase corpus. The connection and field mapping are in [Homebase learning](references/homebase-learning.md). Preserve what actually happened; an untried possibility remains an open question. Respect read-only and discussion-only boundaries.

When asked to grow the prompt collection, update its canonical KB note and source links; keep the published page a separately owned projection. Preserve exact prompts or label adaptations, add a short meaning line where useful, and merge equivalent wording. Keep application guidance in this skill rather than expanding each prompt into a procedure. An interesting question may enter as an adaptation before field use; claim practical benefit only when a real case supports it. Executable skills and Playbooks retain their own adoption and delivery rules.

Finish with what became visible, the concrete change in approach, and the next useful opening. When a case was captured, link its readable entry. When nothing worth retaining emerged, leave the corpus unchanged.
