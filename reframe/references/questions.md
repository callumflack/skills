# Prompt collection and further questions

## Start with the prompt collection

Callum’s canonical collection is `PROMTP REFRAMING — List E7.md` in the KB’s inner `wiki/` folder. Preserve that existing filename. Resolve Homebase through `CALLUM_HOMEBASE` or `~/Repos/callumflack/homebase`, then open `KB/kb-2026/wiki/PROMTP REFRAMING — List E7.md`. Read the collection itself before selecting a prompt. Its three sections reconsider the task, what feels necessary, and how to meet the world; they are ways into the material, not stages to complete.

The [published Prompt framing page](https://www.callum.website/prompt-framing?from=writing) is a reading fallback when the KB is unavailable. It may lag local edits. If neither is accessible, state that gap and use the supplied context or further questions below without claiming to have read the collection.

The collection owns original prompts and labelled adaptations, including Gwern’s “Could a human working for free do it?” and the Dan Luu lens under “What has become worth building?”. This skill owns selection and application. Develop whichever perspective fits the actual situation; do not ask the user to choose among prompt names.

## Further questions

These source-derived questions can help develop a selected perspective or supply one missing from the collection. They can be addressed to the agent, the human, or both. The agent should answer what it can investigate itself. Existing IDs connect questions to cases; they do not impose an order or a checklist. [Sources](sources.md) retains provenance.

### Find a family

| Family | Use when |
| --- | --- |
| Speed | A change in time or cost might change how we work. |
| Altitude | The requested deliverable may stand in for a more useful outcome. |
| Depth | The outcome is right but the method is too shallow. |
| Range | A different field or medium could reveal another approach. |
| Subtraction | Work may be compensating for an obsolete limitation. |
| Agentification | Human effort connects systems, or a valuable responsibility needs a more useful handoff. |
| Desire | We need to choose what deserves attention, or what we want to make. |
| Identity | An inherited role or self-image may narrow what we attempt. |
| Context | A useful distinction is missing from the proposed arrangement. |
| Evidence | We need a way for the world to correct the account. |
| Hunches | Old ideas or unexpected details may have acquired new possibilities. |
| Learning | An attempt could change how we work together next time. |

## Speed

Source: Felix's speed distinction. These adaptations explore what lower time or cost could change.

- **speed-1:** If this took minutes instead of days, how would we organise the work differently?
- **speed-2:** Which steps still assume that attempts, revisions or comparisons are expensive?
- **speed-3:** What would faster execution let us notice or try sooner, and where would the waiting move?

For Luu’s distinct question about which software becomes worth building, open “What has become worth building?” in the canonical prompt collection. [Sources](sources.md#dan-luu) preserves the distinction from accelerating existing work.

## Altitude

Source: Felix's altitude distinction and the KB's loop-engineering synthesis. Use a higher outcome when it serves the actual purpose.

- **altitude-1:** What do we hope happens after this task is finished, and could we work directly on that?
- **altitude-2:** What condition are these repeated requests trying to maintain, and what would let us maintain it continuously?
- **altitude-3:** If we could redesign how this outcome is achieved, which part of the present deliverable would still be necessary?

## Depth

Source: Felix's depth distinction. `depth-1` is the question developed in the originating Callum-agent discussion. These questions seek a deeper version of the task without making it bigger.

- **depth-1:** For this outcome, what better method am I failing to request because I'm assuming the limits of how I previously worked?
- **depth-2:** What would a leading practitioner do here that our present request leaves out?
- **depth-3:** What raw material and actual instruments would let us examine this more directly than the summary we were given?
- **depth-4:** Which accepted explanation could we try to reproduce from the underlying evidence, and what would we learn if it failed?

## Range

Source: Felix's range distinction and the KB's Seven Ways reading. Borrow the method, then investigate how it fits.

- **range-1:** If we describe what this must accomplish with verbs instead of naming the deliverable, which other approaches become available?
- **range-2:** Who handles this kind of difficulty in another field, and what do they actually do?
- **range-3:** Which tool, medium or technique we already have could serve a different purpose here?

## Subtraction

Source: the KB's Agent opportunities inquiry, especially its discussion of removing requirements before accelerating work. These are proposed transfers, not a universal deletion rule.

- **subtraction-1:** Which parts of this work exist only to compensate for limitations we no longer have?
- **subtraction-2:** What could disappear entirely if the underlying information or capability were available at the right moment?
- **subtraction-3:** Which coordination step could we remove by changing who can see or act on the relevant information?

## Agentification

Source: the KB's Agent-native interface stack and Agent opportunities inquiry. Reconsider what computers could carry while preserving meaningful human participation.

- **agentification-1:** Which steps exist because a person currently has to carry context between systems?
- **agentification-2:** What would let an agent observe the relevant state, take a bounded action and see whether it worked?
- **agentification-3:** If this capability became dependable and almost free, what would become worth attempting that we currently rule out?
- **agentification-4:** If this burden disappeared, what worthwhile work would we choose next, and what would the systems need to support it?
- **agentification-5:** [Gwern’s free-human delegation test](sources.md#gwern-branwen). Open the canonical KB entry before using its original or tightened prompt to examine a handoff or find an opportunity.

## Desire

Source: Desire locates the problem and the 10x agency inquiry. The creative question also preserves the originating discussion's interest in making things without requiring an economic justification.

- **desire-1:** Whose actual problem are we trying to change, and where do they encounter it?
- **desire-2:** What would I want to make here if it did not have to justify itself as useful?
- **desire-3:** What am I waiting to finish before I give this work the attention I want it to have?

## Identity

Source: We are how we solve problems. These questions invite examination rather than diagnosing someone's psychology.

- **identity-1:** What would I try here if I did not have to remain the kind of person who usually does this work?
- **identity-2:** Am I protecting the outcome I care about, or a familiar way of being good at it?
- **identity-3:** Which part of doing this myself is meaningful to me, and which part is a habit we could reconsider?

## Context

Source: Agent opportunities' practitioner questions and the existing Homebase cases. `context-2` adapts the readable/editable and recorded-access cases; open the actual case before applying its distinction.

- **context-1:** What did an experienced person notice in the last difficult case that our current description leaves out?
- **context-2:** Which part of this proposal works for the agent while leaving the human unable to participate?
- **context-3:** Show two apparently similar situations that deserve different responses. What difference makes the responses appropriate?

## Evidence

Source: Felix's outside judge, Verification separates compounding memory from rotting memory, and the existing case about testing general claims. Match the check to the claim someone needs to rely on.

- **evidence-1:** What, outside our own account of the work, could show that this approach is right or wrong?
- **evidence-2:** What would we expect to observe if our explanation were mistaken?
- **evidence-3:** What did this attempt establish, and which larger claim are we tempted to make from it?

## Hunches

Source: Seven Ways, Agent opportunities, Attention re-entry, and curious. Stay with a promising possibility long enough to develop it.

- **hunches-1:** Which abandoned idea has just acquired its missing ingredient?
- **hunches-2:** What unexpected detail here becomes more interesting if we follow it instead of explaining it away?
- **hunches-3:** What was I trying to do when I left this thought, and what has changed enough to let me return differently?

## Learning

Source: Human-agent learning across Homebase and the existing cases about access, recording/classification, and complete capture. Use the existing capture workflow for material experience.

- **learning-1:** What did this attempt teach us to notice or ask before the next similar task?
- **learning-2:** Now that we can do this, what becomes worth asking next?
- **learning-3:** Which part of what worked should become a question, a method or a tool, and which part depended on this particular situation?
