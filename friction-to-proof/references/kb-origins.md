## Local Compression

From [[Make the miss visible before asking to be understood — Synthesis E7]]:

- "They don't get it" is the least useful form of the signal.
- The useful object is the proof surface: artifact, check, prototype, example, path, trace, or diff.
- The harder question is: what proof would make the team change the decision without granting special status?

From [[READ Daniel Kazandjian — The Neuroscience of Enlightenment  Kathryn Devaney E7]]:

- High reactivity can pilot action through old forces rather than current goals.
- Past experience creates attentional filters that shape present perception.
- Practice can create prediction errors and prune filters that no longer serve.
- Safety/equanimity is not passivity; it makes action more accurate.

From the taste/know-how cluster:

- Tacit know-how often appears first as taste or friction.
- It must become visible in an artifact before outsiders can evaluate it.
- The artifact is not decoration; it is the proof surface.

## Protocol Implication

The skill must hold two truths at once:

1. Anger may be the fingertip feel of real expertise noticing a miss before language catches up.
2. Anger may also be amplified or misread by old threat filters.

Therefore the protocol order matters:

```text
preserve cue -> build proof -> run signal check -> ask for decision change
```

Do not start with wound analysis. That turns the sensor off.

Do not skip signal check. That lets old filters drive the car.
